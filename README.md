# AudioPatch Community

Welcome to the public space for questions, ideas, and feedback about AudioPatch, a macOS app for audio routing and virtual microphones.

## Join the discussion

Start in [Discussions](https://github.com/techprimate/audiopatch-community/discussions). Search for an existing conversation before posting, then choose the category that fits:

| Category | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Q&A | Questions about using AudioPatch or getting help with setup. |
| Ideas | Feature suggestions and improvements. Describe what you want to accomplish and why. |
| General | Product feedback and topics that do not fit elsewhere. |
| Announcements | Updates from the AudioPatch team. |
| Polls | Community votes and questions from the team. |
| Show and tell | Sharing how you use AudioPatch. |

If you find a similar discussion, add your experience or upvote it instead of opening a duplicate. We read feedback, but cannot promise a response to every post or that a suggested feature will be built.

## Report a problem

Open a discussion in Q&A or General and include what you expected, what happened, your macOS and AudioPatch versions, and steps to reproduce the problem. For virtual microphone problems, mention whether the separate plug-in is installed. Do not post recordings, graph files, logs, or other sensitive information publicly.

For private support or privacy requests, email [support@techprimate.com](mailto:support@techprimate.com). The app also has an in-app feedback form.

The virtual microphone requires a separate plug-in installer. Only use download links published by techprimate GmbH, not links posted by other community members.
